### 1. Explainable and Cross-Dataset RUL Prediction for Turbofan Engines
- **Status:** Unpublished Research
- **Working Title:** Machine Learning for Remaining Useful Life Prediction of Turbofan Engines: A Lightweight, Explainable, and Cross-Dataset Generalization Study
- **Research Area:** Predictive Maintenance, Machine Learning and Explainable AI
- **Benchmarks / Testbed:** NASA C-MAPSS FD001 & FD003 (Single Operating Condition, Distinct Fault Modes)
- **Central Question:** How reliably can models estimate equipment remaining useful life across fault modes?
- **Approaches & Methods:** NASA C-MAPSS · FD001 / FD003 · Random Forest · XGBoost · LSTM · SHAP

### 2. Decision-Aware Uncertainty for Maintenance under Operating Shift
- **Status:** Unpublished Research
- **Working Title:** Does Uncertainty-Aware Prognostics Actually Reduce Maintenance Cost Under Operating-Condition Shift? A Decision-Theoretic Evaluation Framework with Cross-Domain Evidence
- **Research Area:** Prognostics, Uncertainty Quantification and Decision Science
- **Benchmarks / Testbed:** NASA C-MAPSS Turbofan Data · FEMTO / PRONOSTIA Bearing Data
- **Central Question:** Do uncertainty estimates lead to better maintenance actions under operating shift?
- **Approaches & Methods:** DAPE · C-MAPSS · FEMTO / PRONOSTIA · ETCE · Tail-Brier · DRC

### 3. Decision-Centric Demand Forecasting for Inventory Replenishment
- **Status:** Unpublished Research
- **Working Title:** Beyond Forecast Accuracy: Decision-Centric Evaluation of Demand Forecasting for Inventory Replenishment under Intermittent and Shifting Retail Demand
